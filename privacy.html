1. Introduction

Eye Gymnastics ("App", "we", "our", "us") is developed and operated by Fazogirapps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, who else is involved, and what choices you have.

By downloading, installing, or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Contact: fazogirapps@gmail.com

2. No Account, No Personal Identity

The App has no registration and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, location, contacts, calendar, photos, camera images, health records, or advertising identifiers.

Because there is nothing identifying you, we cannot connect App usage to you as a person.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Everything the App remembers about your practice is written to a local database on your device and never leaves it, unless your own device backup (iCloud or Google backup) includes it. That data is:

  • which exercises and routines you completed, and when;
  • your current streak and your longest streak;
  • routines you create or customise;
  • your settings: reminder times, sound and haptic preferences, and onboarding answers;
  • a flag recording whether premium features are unlocked.

We have no server that receives this data and no way to read it remotely.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Every exercise in the App is free. Eye Gymnastics offers an optional auto-renewing subscription that unlocks features built around the exercises, currently your full practice history and statistics, the guided programs, the complete achievement set, and unlimited daily reminders. See section 9 of the Terms of Use for the current list.

We never see or handle your card details. Payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchases and tell the App whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user identifier it generates itself, the purchase receipt issued by the store, and basic device and country information.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

7. Device Permissions

  • Notifications — only to deliver the practice reminders you set up. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no push server is involved, and we send nothing to your phone ourselves.
  • Exact alarms and start on boot (Android) — so that a reminder still arrives at the minute you chose, and survives a restart.
  • Vibration — for the haptic cues during an exercise, which you can switch off in Settings.

The App does not request camera, microphone, photo library, location, or contacts access. You can withdraw notification permission at any time in your device settings; the rest of the App keeps working.

8. Data Retention

  • On your device — kept until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes the local database completely.
  • Firebase Analytics — event data is retained by Google for a limited period under our project's retention setting, then deleted automatically.
  • Crashlytics — crash reports are retained by Google for approximately 90 days.
  • RevenueCat — purchase records are kept while the subscription and its history are relevant to the stores.

9. Your Rights and Choices

  • Erase everything the App stored — uninstall the App. There is no cloud copy to delete.
  • Stop the analytics identifier — uninstalling resets it; on iOS you may also disable it in your device's privacy settings for the App where offered.
  • Cancel your subscription — in your App Store or Google Play settings. Uninstalling the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Ask us — write to fazogirapps@gmail.com. Because we hold no account and no identifying data, we may be unable to locate any record relating to you specifically.
