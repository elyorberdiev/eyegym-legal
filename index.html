Eye Gymnastics

Legal documents for the Eye Gymnastics mobile app (com.fazogirapps.eyegym), available on iOS and Android.
Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Eye Gymnastics guides you through short eye-exercise routines designed as a break from prolonged screen work. It works without an account: there is no sign-in, and your practice history, streaks, and routines stay on your device.

Please note: Eye Gymnastics is a general wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any eye condition and will not correct your vision. Stop immediately if an exercise causes pain, dizziness, or visual disturbance, and consult an eye-care professional. See section 3 and the photosensitivity warning in the Terms of Use.
